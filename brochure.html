⚠️ The Unmet Clinical Problem

Emergency departments rely on routine unenhanced NCCT as the universal frontline scan. But contrast-enhanced CTA is only ordered for ~10% of patients. In community hospitals and night shifts, subtle 2–5 mm aneurysms on thick-slice (3–5 mm) scans are overlooked. When a missed aneurysm ruptures, mortality is 40–50%, and hospital care exceeds $250,000 per survivor. Prior CAD algorithms failed because normal tortuous bends (MCA Sylvian genu) and bifurcation carinas triggered hundreds of false alarms.

💡 The NeuroVasc AI Solution

NeuroVasc AI combines topological spherical-shell decomposition with scale-calibrated differential geometry and zero-torsion Bishop parallel transport neural networks. By analyzing cross-sectional attenuation blooming rather than scalar centerline curvature, the system isolates true saccular outpouchings while ignoring normal tortuous bends. Restricting inference to Top-3 spatial candidates eliminates multiple-testing penalties and ensures rapid triage.

The Proprietary Multi-Modal Cascade

1 Bounded Annular Shell (1.5mm < r ≤ 3.5mm)

Evaluates 8-octant angular attenuation asymmetry, detecting directional partial-volume blooming while filtering symmetric cylindrical vessel lumens.

2 Scale-Calibrated Curvature (κ / κref)

Normalizes metric curvature against vessel radius using a hyperbolic screening boundary, cleanly separating normal Sylvian genu tortuosity from saccular pathology.

3 Zero-Torsion Bishop Transport Frame

Constructs parallel transport frames along 3D vessel manifolds with zero mathematical torsion, extracting true cross-sectional patches without Frenet twist.

4 Cascaded Spatial Gating (Top-3 Clustering)

Limits patient-level inference to Top 3 candidate loci via a Gradient-Boosted Decision Tree (GBDT), suppressing 99% of multiple-testing false alarm opportunities.
